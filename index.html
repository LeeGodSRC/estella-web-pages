Story

The Golden Comet

Players will follow a golden comet from a mysterious universe that has been protecting different planets since ancient times. Whenever it passes across the sky, it ignites new stories in some corner of the universe.

Among these countless stories, a mysterious purple force lurks in the darkness, gradually changing the order of the universe. Each story begins with a protagonist chosen by the comet, who gradually explores and unravels various extraordinary events on the planet.
